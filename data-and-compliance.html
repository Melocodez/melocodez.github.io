DriveSync

Data & Compliance · Effective November 11, 2026

Overview

This page sums up how DriveSync handles data and which rules it follows. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

At a glance

  • No accounts, no sign-in and no DriveSync servers
  • No analytics, and no selling of personal information
  • Everything you save stays on your iPhone
  • DriveSync never asks to track you
  • The free version shows muted Google ads, never on the car screen; No Ads removes them

App Store privacy label

Because of Google's ad software in the free version, DriveSync's App Store privacy label lists these as "Data Not Linked to You": Coarse Location, Device ID, Product Interaction, Advertising Data, Crash Data, Performance Data and Other Diagnostic Data, used for Third-Party Advertising and Analytics. Nothing is listed as "Data Used to Track You". Everything DriveSync saves itself stays on your iPhone and isn't collected.

Permissions and why

  • Location (While Using the App): to show places near you in Places. Optional; you can type any address instead.
  • Camera: only when you tap Scan, to read an address. Optional.
  • Local Network: only while you cast to a car or screen with a web browser. Optional.
  • Live Activities: to show the place on your car screen. You can turn it off.
  • Paste: iOS asks each time DriveSync reads what you've copied.
  • Photos: no permission needed. The iOS photo picker shares only the photo you pick.
  • Tracking: never asked. DriveSync doesn't use App Tracking Transparency.

Where data is kept, and for how long

  • Favorites, recently used websites, sent places and settings: on your iPhone until you clear them or delete the app
  • Car screen card: up to 8 hours, then it ends
  • Cache (icons, pages, downloads): on your iPhone until you or iOS clear it
  • Recent place searches: in memory for up to 30 minutes
  • Camera images, photos, pasted text and your screen while casting: never stored
  • Ad information: held by Google under its policies

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Privacy › Clear Cache: icons, cached pages and recent place searches, without signing you out of websites
  • Settings › Privacy › Clear Browsing Data: cookies, website data, cache and recently used websites
  • Settings › Privacy › Clear History & Favorites: sent places, recently used websites and favorites
  • Deleting DriveSync: removes everything it stored on your iPhone
  • Ad information: Ad Privacy Choices in Settings (where shown), or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com

Third-party software

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK with Google's User Messaging Platform: ads and ad consent. It includes Google's own privacy manifest.
  • Apple frameworks: MapKit, StoreKit, ActivityKit, ReplayKit, VisionKit, AVFoundation, WebKit and Declared Age Range.

DriveSync contains no other third-party analytics, tracking or advertising software. It includes a privacy manifest that declares the iOS features it uses and why.

Privacy laws

DriveSync is designed to follow:

  • Apple's App Store Review Guidelines on privacy, data use and sharing (section 5.1)
  • EU GDPR, UK GDPR and Swiss data protection law: Google's consent form appears in those regions before any personalized ads
  • US state privacy laws, such as those in California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon
  • COPPA: DriveSync isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information

Age rating and age laws

DriveSync is rated 16+ on the App Store because it includes a web browser with unrestricted web access. In places with app store age laws, such as Texas, Utah and Louisiana, DriveSync uses Apple's Declared Age Range feature: people under 16 can't use it, and for people aged 16 to 17 Safe Search is set to Strict and ads aren't personalized.

Driver safety

  • The car screen shows only a place's name and address: no browsing, video or typing
  • Ads never appear on the car screen and are always muted
  • DriveSync asks you to set destinations before you drive

Encryption and export

DriveSync uses only the standard encryption built into iOS, such as HTTPS, and declares this to Apple. It contains no proprietary encryption.

Accessibility

DriveSync supports larger text (Dynamic Type), VoiceOver labels, Dark Mode and high-contrast colors.
