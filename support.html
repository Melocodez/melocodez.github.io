The address doesn't appear on my car screen

DriveSync shows places on CarPlay as a Live Activity, which needs iOS 26 or later.

1. On your iPhone, open Settings › Apps › DriveSync and make sure Live Activities is on.
2. On your car's screen, open Settings and make sure Live Activities is on.
3. Send a place before you start driving. The card appears briefly at the bottom of the car screen, then stays on the CarPlay dashboard (swipe to the first CarPlay page).

In DriveSync, Home › CarPlay Setup checks these for you and can send a test card.

Sending an address from another app, a note or a photo

Open the Places tab. Under "Already have an address?": tap Paste after copying an address anywhere, Scan to read a written or printed address with the camera, or Photo to pick a screenshot or photo with an address in it.

Scan needs camera access. If you said no, turn it on in iPhone Settings › Apps › DriveSync › Camera.

Ads and No Ads

DriveSync is free with ads: a small banner above the tab bar, and a full-screen ad at most once every 10 minutes when you open the app. Ads are always muted, hide while you cast, and never appear on your car screen. To learn how ads are chosen or to report one, open Settings › About Ads. The No Ads subscription ($1.99/week, $4.99/month or $24.99/year) removes all ads. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open Settings › No Ads › Restore Purchases. To cancel, open Settings › Manage Subscription, or your Apple Account settings.

Cast to Screen

Shows your iPhone screen on a car display or any screen with a web browser, such as a Tesla.

1. Turn on Personal Hotspot on your iPhone and connect the car to it (or put both on the same Wi-Fi).
2. In DriveSync, tap Cast to Screen, then Start Broadcast.
3. Using a Tesla or another car with a web browser? Tap Using a Tesla? under Cast to Screen and type the address it shows into the car's browser.

Your destination pops up full screen so it's easy to read. To stop, tap Cast to Screen again, or the red indicator at the top of your iPhone. Turn on Do Not Disturb so notifications don't show on the car screen. Regular CarPlay screens can't show casting; use Send to Car for those.
