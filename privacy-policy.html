DriveSync

Privacy Policy · Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

DriveSync has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. We don't collect, receive or sell your personal information, and what you save in DriveSync stays on your iPhone. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some information to show and measure ads, as explained below. The No Ads subscription removes them.

Who we are

DriveSync is made by Catherine Garcia ("we" or "us"). For people in the EU, UK and Switzerland, we're the "controller" of the limited information this policy describes. Contact us at camilomarvin0@gmail.com or by mail at 16 Academy Terrace, Linden, NJ 07036.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you reaches us. DriveSync doesn't send us your searches, places, favorites, location, photos or browsing, and it contains no analytics. Some features work through other companies (Apple, Google and DuckDuckGo), which receive information directly from your iPhone under their own privacy policies. Each one is described below.

Information kept on your iPhone

DriveSync stores your favorites, recently used websites, the places you send to your car, and your settings on your iPhone only. None of this is sent to us. You can remove it anytime in DriveSync › Settings › Privacy, or by deleting the app.

Cache

To load faster and use less mobile data, DriveSync keeps temporary copies of website icons, web pages and other downloads on your iPhone, like any browser. Recent place search results are kept in memory for up to 30 minutes and never saved to storage. Recent Sounds results are kept for up to 24 hours. The cache never leaves your iPhone. Clear it anytime in DriveSync › Settings › Privacy › Clear Cache. iOS may also clear it when your iPhone is low on storage.

Cookies

DriveSync itself doesn't use cookies. Websites you open in DriveSync's browser may set their own cookies, just like in Safari. Remove them anytime with DriveSync › Settings › Privacy › Clear Browsing Data.

Location

If you allow it, DriveSync uses your location only while you search in Places, to show results near you. Place searches are handled by Apple Maps (MapKit) under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive or store your location. You can turn location off in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › DriveSync. You can still search for any place or address without it.

Camera, photos and copied text

DriveSync uses these only when you tap Scan, Photo or Paste in Places, to find an address and show it on your car screen. Scan uses the camera only while the scanner is open, and iOS asks for your permission first. Photo uses the iOS photo picker, so DriveSync only sees the one photo you choose and never has access to your photo library. Paste reads only what you've copied, at the moment you tap it, and iOS shows a paste prompt. Text is read on your iPhone with Apple's built-in text recognition. Images and text are never saved or sent anywhere. You can turn off camera access anytime in iPhone Settings › Apps › DriveSync.

Web search and websites

Searches you type are sent to DuckDuckGo to show results, under DuckDuckGo's Privacy Policy. Websites you open receive the same information they would from any browser, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Website icons are loaded from DuckDuckGo's icon service using only the website's domain name (for example, "espn.com").

Sounds

Sounds uses YouTube API Services. When you search in Sounds, what you type is sent to YouTube (owned by Google) through the YouTube Data API to find matching videos, and videos play in YouTube's embedded player, under Google's Privacy Policy. YouTube's player receives the same information it would on any website, such as your IP address, may use cookies or similar technologies, and may show YouTube's own ads in the player. DriveSync shows no ads of its own in Sounds. So repeat searches load instantly and don't use up Sounds' daily searches, your recent Sounds searches and their results (video titles, channels, thumbnails and lengths) are kept on your iPhone for up to 24 hours, then deleted; Clear Cache removes them sooner. They're never sent to us, and Sounds never uses your Google account. By using Sounds, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Your car screen

When you tap Send to Car, the place name and address are shown in a Live Activity on your iPhone and, when your iPhone is connected, on your car's CarPlay screen, with a small picture: a map of the place from Apple Maps, a snapshot of the web page you were viewing, or the video's thumbnail. The picture is kept only on your iPhone and replaced the next time you send something. This happens entirely on your iPhone; DriveSync doesn't use a server for it. To show CONNECTED on Home, DriveSync checks on your iPhone whether its audio is going to CarPlay. That check isn't saved or sent anywhere. You can turn off Live Activities in iPhone Settings › Apps › DriveSync › Live Activities.

Cast to Screen

When you tap Cast to Screen and start a broadcast in iOS's Screen Sharing panel, iOS records everything on your iPhone screen, including notifications, until you stop it. DriveSync sends that picture only from your iPhone, directly over your Personal Hotspot or local Wi-Fi, to the screen whose browser opens your private DriveSync link. It never goes over the internet or to us, isn't saved, and has no sound. This local connection isn't encrypted, and anyone on the same network who knows your private link could see your screen while you cast, so only cast on networks you trust, such as your own Personal Hotspot. Turn on Do Not Disturb to keep notifications off the car screen. Stop anytime from the Screen Sharing panel or the red indicator at the top of your iPhone.

Screen mirroring

If you mirror your iPhone with AirPlay or a cable instead, DriveSync shows only the current destination's name and address on that screen.

Ads

If you don't have No Ads, DriveSync shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner above the tab bar, and a full-screen ad at most once every 10 minutes, when you open or come back to the app or when you switch tabs. Ads hide while you cast your screen. To show ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and measure results, Google's software in DriveSync may collect:

  • your IP address, and your approximate location based on it
  • device and app information, such as iPhone model, iOS version, language and app version
  • identifiers iOS lets apps use without asking (not your device's advertising identifier)
  • how you interact with ads, and crash and performance information

DriveSync doesn't ask to track you, so iOS doesn't give Google or us your device's advertising identifier, and DriveSync doesn't combine information about you with information from other companies' apps or websites. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change it anytime in DriveSync › Settings › Ad Privacy Choices. People aged 16 to 17 only see non-personalized ads. Ads never appear on your car screen and are always muted. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The No Ads subscription is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details or Apple Account information. DriveSync only checks with Apple, on your iPhone, whether your subscription is active, so it can turn ads off.

Age range

In places where the law requires it, such as Texas, Utah and Louisiana, DriveSync asks iOS for your age range using Apple's Declared Age Range feature. DriveSync only receives a range, such as "16 to 17" or "18 or older", never your birthdate. It's used only on your iPhone: people under 16 can't use DriveSync, and for people under 18 Safe Search is set to Strict and ads aren't personalized. Your age range isn't stored or sent to us.

Who receives information

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone except as described here:

  • Apple: place searches (Apple Maps), purchases, Live Activities and age range, under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google: the ad information listed above, and Sounds searches and videos (YouTube API Services), under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • DuckDuckGo: web searches you type and website domain names for icons, under DuckDuckGo's Privacy Policy.
  • Websites you open: what any browser sends, under each website's own policy.
  • The law: we'd share information only if the law requires it. Because we don't hold your data, we'd have little or nothing to share.

Legal bases (EU, UK and Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for personalized ads and for camera, location and paste access (you give it in the Google and iOS prompts and can withdraw it anytime); on our contract with you for the No Ads subscription; and on our legitimate interests in keeping DriveSync free with non-personalized ads, preventing fraud and keeping the app secure.

Keeping and deleting data

We don't have a server, so we don't keep your data. Data on your iPhone stays there until you clear it in DriveSync › Settings › Privacy or delete the app. The car screen card ends after 8 hours. Information Google collects for ads is kept under Google's policies; you can limit it with Ad Privacy Choices (where shown) or by getting No Ads.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to how it's used, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising.

  • Everyone: you control location, camera and Live Activities in iPhone Settings, and can clear everything DriveSync stores in DriveSync › Settings › Privacy or by deleting the app.
  • EU, UK and Switzerland: you have the rights in the GDPR, UK GDPR and Swiss data protection law, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and Oregon): we don't sell personal information for money. Showing ads with Google may count as "selling" or "sharing" for targeted advertising under some of these laws. You can opt out in DriveSync › Settings › Ad Privacy Choices where it's shown, or by getting No Ads. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

To make a request, email camilomarvin0@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days, or sooner if your local law requires it. Because we don't hold your data, we'll explain what's stored on your iPhone and help you reach Google or Apple for anything they hold. You can appeal our answer by replying to it; if you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general or data protection authority.

Security

Information on your iPhone is protected by iOS, including encryption when your iPhone is locked with a passcode. DriveSync connects to Apple, Google and DuckDuckGo over encrypted HTTPS. Cast to Screen uses only your local network and isn't encrypted, so only cast on networks you trust. No system is perfectly secure, but because we don't keep your data on servers, there's nothing of yours for us to lose in a data breach.

International users

Apple, Google and DuckDuckGo may process information in the United States and other countries, using the safeguards described in their own policies, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
